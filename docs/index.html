elixr-casestudy01

Rendered reports for a serological survey workflow that collects multiple plate files with {plater}, standardizes Antibody Units across plates (Miura K, 2008), and classifies seropositivity from Antibody Units (Sepúlveda N, 2015).

These reports document the analysis workflow used for Guerra Manihuari, C. A. (2024). Seropositividad de toxoplasmosis en pacientes VIH empleando unidades arbitrarias de anticuerpos [Tesis, Universidad Nacional de la Amazonía Peruana]. Available at the UNAP institutional repository.
  1. 01 · Test plate template Reading multiple plate layouts with {plater}.
  2. 02 · Standardize plates Standardizing Antibody Units across plates.
  3. 03 · Estimate antibody units Dose-response curve fitting for a single plate.
  4. 04 · Estimate antibody units (purrr) Iterating the estimation across all plates with purrr.
  5. 05 · Serological classification Classifying seropositivity from Antibody Units.
  6. 06 · Compare classification Comparing classification methods.
